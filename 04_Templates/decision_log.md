# Decision Log

## Purpose

Record significant project decisions and the reasoning behind them.

Use this log only for decisions that materially affect the project,
such as scope, approach, assumptions, system configuration, schedule,
responsibilities, or major technical choices.

Do not use it for routine actions, meeting notes, or minor operational decisions.

## Usage Rules

Record a decision when it:

- changes or clarifies project scope;
- affects the execution approach;
- establishes an important assumption or constraint;
- selects or rejects a relevant alternative;
- changes a major responsibility, milestone, or schedule condition;
- affects subsequent technical or management decisions.

Keep each entry concise.

Record only completed decisions. Pending decisions belong in the attention
section of `project_status.md` when that document is used.

The decision log records **what was decided and why**. Detailed analysis,
evidence, calculations, or meeting discussions should remain in their
corresponding project documents.

When a previous decision is changed, create a new entry rather than
rewriting the historical record, and reference the superseded decision ID.
